Details

Description:
Our intense Repair Conditioner hydrates and strengthens hair. The Silk Protien adds moisture to hair and helps prevent split ends and frizz, leaving hair feeling beautiful, strong, soft, and healthy with a natural shine.

Benefits:
  • Seals in moisture for ultimate shine
  • Reduces frizz
  • Makes hair stronger
  • Reduces Split Ends
  • Makes hair soft and Manageable
  • Easy to comb and style
  • Enhanced with Aloe vera and Biotin

Usage: Apply a small amount to clean, wet hair. Leave on for 3 minutes and rinse.

Skin Type: All Hair types
Ingredients : DM Water, TMC-85, BTMS-50,  Glycerine, EDTA, Aloe Vera Concentrate, Hibiscus Extract, Vitamin B5 ( Panthenol), Cetyl Alcohol, Preservative.

Directions :
Apply a small amount to clean, wet hair. Leave on for 3 minutes and rinse.
